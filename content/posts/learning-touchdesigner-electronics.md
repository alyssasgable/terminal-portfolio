For about a year I’ve been learning TouchDesigner and finding ways to explore creative coding. 

Recently, I’ve gained an interest in building my own electronics to control my visuals to make them more interactive. I got an Arduino and beginner electronics kit to start learning the basics of electronics. 

I want to understand how physical components like sensors and other electronic inputs can communicate with software. I'm also exploring how audio signals and Python can become part of that process.

Rather than learning everything separately, I want to build small experiments that combine electronics, programming, and visual design.

The bigger goal is to create interactive installations and live visuals where the technology and the experience work together.

This actually is a full circle moment for me because one of the hardest classes I had in college was Digital Logic Circuits! A few years later and I’m finally understanding what all those crazy lines meant. This was another testament to learning styles. I had the hardest time understanding circuits from diagrams but once I started learning from a practical hands on approach I was finally able to make sense of things  :).
